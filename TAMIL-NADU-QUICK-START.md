## How to Use

### Step 1: Open the App
Visit your deployed URL (Vercel/Netlify)

### Step 2: Enter Location
1. Click "Enter location manually"
2. Paste coordinates from above
3. Click "Search"

### Step 3: See Results
- View nearby EV charging stations
- See distance, price, and availability
- Select a station to proceed
